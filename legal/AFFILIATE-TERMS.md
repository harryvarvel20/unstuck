## 1. What this is

You (the "Creator") promote ADHV to your audience using a unique discount code
that gives your audience **10% off** an ADHV Pro subscription. These terms
govern that arrangement. They exist mainly to keep both of us on the right
side of UK advertising and consumer law — the ASA holds **both** the creator
and the brand responsible for a non-compliant ad.

## 2. Disclosure — non-negotiable

UK law (Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations) and the CAP Code
require that paid or incentivised promotion is **obvious to the audience
before they engage with it**.

You must:

- Include **#ad** (or **#advert**) **prominently and up front** — in the first
  line of the caption, not buried in a hashtag block, not below a "more" fold,
  and stated verbally near the start of video or audio content.
- Use the platform's own paid-partnership tool as well, where one exists. The
  platform label alone is **not** sufficient — the #ad still needs to be there.
- Disclose on **every** post, story, short and repost — not just the first one.
- Not describe the relationship as merely a "collab", "partner", "sp", "spon",
  "thanks to", or an affiliate-link-only mention.

This applies whether you are paid in money, commission, free access, or gifted
product. **If you are unsure whether something needs a disclosure, disclose
it.**

## 3. Claims you must not make

ADHV is a **self-management and skills tool — not therapy, diagnosis, or
medical treatment**, and it is not a medical device. You must not say or imply
that ADHV:

- treats, cures, manages, or is a treatment for ADHD or any other condition;
- is an alternative to medication, therapy, or professional healthcare;
- can diagnose ADHD or tell someone whether they have it;
- produces guaranteed outcomes ("this will fix your focus", "you'll never
  procrastinate again", "cured my ADHD");
- is endorsed by any clinician, health body, or charity unless we have given
  you that in writing.

You must not present personal results as typical, disparage competitors
unfairly, or target your promotion at under-18s. ADHV is an 18+ service.

**Do say things like:** "an app that breaks tasks into tiny steps and sits
with you while you do them", "it helps me start", "this is what works for my
brain". Speak from your own experience, honestly.

## 4. Your code

- Each Creator gets a unique code; it gives the customer **10% off**.
- Don't post the code to coupon-aggregator or deal sites.
- Don't run paid search ads on "ADHV" or misspellings of it, and don't imply
  you are ADHV or speak on our behalf.
- We can deactivate a code at any time, and will do so immediately if these
  terms are breached.

## 5. Commission and payment

Commission rate, term, and payment schedule are agreed separately in writing
with each Creator. Payments follow the agreed schedule after any refund or
chargeback window. No commission is payable on fraudulent, self-referred, or
incentivised-signup conversions.

## 6. Content, IP and data

You keep ownership of your content. You grant ADHV permission to reshare it on
our own channels with credit. You may use the ADHV name and logo only to
promote ADHV, unmodified. Don't share screenshots containing another person's
private content, and don't show a child's face, name or school in any ADHV
content.

## 7. Compliance, ending, and liability

You are responsible for complying with the law and platform rules in your
territory, and for any tax on your earnings. Either of us may end the
arrangement at any time with written notice; disclosure obligations continue
to apply to content that stays online. If a breach of these terms causes ADHV
a loss or a regulatory issue, we may recover it from you. These terms are
governed by the law of England and Wales.
