## The Brief — send this with the terms

Creators need to know what to _say_, not just what not to say. Give them this
and they will produce better content and fewer compliance problems.

### What ADHV is, in one line

> An app that breaks a task you have been avoiding into tiny steps, then sits
> with you while you do the first one.

### Angles that work

- **The wall.** "I know what I need to do. I cannot start." That is the
  feeling the product exists for — lead with it.
- **The first step is absurdly small.** "Put your shoes by the door." Show it.
  It is the most convincing thing about the product.
- **Body double.** A visible timer and gentle check-ins so you are not doing
  it alone.
- **Time Truth.** It learns how long things actually take _you_, rather than
  telling you off for being slow.
- **A bad day button.** One tap salvages what matters and lets the rest go.

### Say it like this

✅ "an app that breaks tasks into tiny steps and sits with you"
✅ "it helps me start"
✅ "this is what works for my brain"
✅ "I still take my meds / still see my therapist — this is a different thing"

### Do not say

❌ "treats / manages / cures ADHD"
❌ "better than medication" · "you won't need therapy"
❌ "it can tell you if you have ADHD"
❌ "you'll never procrastinate again"
❌ anything pairing ADHV with crisis, self-harm or suicide content

### Disclosure — the bit that gets people fined

- **#ad in the first line**, not in a hashtag block at the bottom
- **Said out loud** near the start of video or audio
- Platform paid-partnership tool **as well**, not instead
- **Every** post, story, short and repost — not just the first

## Step 4 — check the disclosure within 24 hours

Look at the actual post, on the actual platform, on a phone.

- [ ] Is **#ad** visible **without tapping "more"**?
- [ ] In video, is it **said aloud** in the first few seconds?
- [ ] Any medical claim, however casual? ("this fixed my ADHD" in a voiceover
      counts)
- [ ] Anything implying it replaces medication or therapy?
- [ ] Does the code work? **Test it yourself.**

If something is wrong, message them the same day and ask for an edit or a
re-post. A quiet fix on day one is nothing; the same content still live in
three months is what a complaint attaches to.
